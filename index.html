Canopy Tag

A fangame inspired by Gorilla Tag. Grip your controllers and pull yourself through the trees — no thumbsticks, just arms.

Casual Free roam the forest, collect bananas, no pressure.
Infection Tag One gorilla starts infected. Get tagged and you're it too. Survive.
Your browser doesn't report WebXR support — open this page in the Oculus/Meta Quest Browser over HTTPS.
In headset: no buttons — just swing your arms like you're pulling yourself along, and you'll move the opposite way. Stop moving your hands to coast to a stop. There's no snap-turn: turn your real body to change direction, just like the real game.

On desktop (preview only): WASD to move, drag mouse to look. This is just for testing layout — put on the headset for the real thing.
